# Reference sheet — TunasPohon

## Brief and scope
- Subject: a generic broadleaf tree growing from a seed to a mature tree.
- Format: vertical 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 90 seconds.
- This is an educational visualisation, not a depiction of a named species. Leaf shape, crown shape, exact branching pattern, and growth times vary widely among tree species.
- Visual direction: warm natural daylight, a visible soil cross-section, connected woody trunk/branches, branching roots below ground, and a progressively wider leafy crown. Keep the same tree identity through all stages.

## Visual specification

### Germination
- Seed coat swells after absorbing water.
- The first root (radicle) emerges and grows downward.
- The embryonic shoot grows upward through the soil.
- Do not show leaves appearing before the shoot emerges.

### Seedling
- Show the young shoot above the soil.
- Seed leaves/cotyledons appear first; true leaves develop afterward.
- Keep the stem slender and green at this stage.
- Roots branch below the soil as the plant establishes itself.

### Sapling / young tree
- Stem becomes woody and thicker.
- Height increases; lateral branches begin to develop.
- Crown remains relatively narrow compared with the mature tree.
- Roots continue to branch and spread.

### Mature tree
- Trunk is visibly thicker and supports a branching crown.
- Canopy is broader and composed of connected foliage masses with individual leaf details.
- Root system is established and branched.
- No species-specific fruit, flower, bark pattern, or leaf arrangement is claimed.

## Motion and continuity
- Growth is shown as a continuous progression, not as unrelated tree images.
- Roots develop alongside the trunk and crown.
- Gentle branch/leaf sway is secondary motion; trunk and roots remain structurally stable.
- Rain is an environmental beat, not a claim that rain alone causes growth.
- Seasonal/time passage is implied artistically; the animation does not assign a universal number of years to each stage.
